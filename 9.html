The Portrait

A face in profile, caught between centuries. The light falls from the left, as it always has in rooms where someone is thinking and the world outside has been asked to wait.

The Red Sea

Not water but ink. Not waves but sentences, each one rising to break against the margin and falling back to begin again, carrying the same salt, the same story.

The Study

A machine sits by the window, still warm. The page holds the indentation of keys that struck hours ago but sound, still, in the mind of whoever passed the door.

The Cottage

Someone lives here. The door is painted white, the roses climb without permission, and the path leads only inward, toward a room that smells of paper and rain.

The Garden

Hollyhocks and delphiniums mark the border between what is cultivated and what simply grew while no one was watching. A bee moves from silence to silence.
