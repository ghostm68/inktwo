Xenophon, a soldier, historian, and student of Socrates, wrote his manual as a practical guide for cavalrymen. But he was also a philosopher, and his writing on horses is tinged with Socratic ethics: the rider must understand his horse's psychology, must treat it as a partner, must earn its trust.
