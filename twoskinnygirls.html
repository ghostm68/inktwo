Manuscript I Obituary EP
Track Listing
CASE I
Broken Vows
Anonymous 8th c. Irish Adaptation — Chords: Am-F-G-C
A translation of grief across twelve centuries. The speaker addresses a lover who promised gold ships and silk suits — promises as impossible as deciphering the dead.
CASE II
Travel
Original Composition — April 2026
A psychosexual road poem through Seoul, Camelot, and the infrared south. Olivia Hussey makes a cameo with velcro bullets in her vest.
CASE III
The Drowning Machine
Dedicated to Bobby Weir — 1972
A meditation on legacy and the Grateful Dead. "He left a lot more than he took." Picasso was never born, and the music never stopped.

Chronology Discography
A Brief History
2024
The Foundation
Two Skinny Girls emerge with Hollywoodland — recorded July 2024, chords scribbled on napkins, the first transmission.
2025
The Artemis Mission
The NASA boarding pass era. Eclectic rock and roll reaches for the moon. Tracks: Decca Mercury, Armageddon, Orgasm.
May 2026
The Obituary EP
Five tracks recorded across two days in April. Broken Vows adapts an 8th century Irish poem. The Drowning Machine tips its hat to Bobby Weir.
June 2026
The Smoke Throne Sessions
Six tracking sessions in one week. Keira, Emilee, Alivia, and Murphy the cat. Working title: "Semen." Final title: still settling.
