Keyboard Shortcuts

  • ? or Ctrl+/ — Open this help
  • Esc — Close help / exit Zen mode
  • Ctrl+Z — Undo
  • Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y — Redo
  • Ctrl+S — Force save current sheet
  • Alt+Z — Toggle Zen mode
  • Focus a knob, then use ↑ ↓ or [ ] to adjust

Other Notes

  • Images are automatically downscaled to preserve local storage.
  • Marquee pauses while you hover it.
  • Sheets are saved locally in your browser.
  • PDF respects current font, size, line pitch, and image ratios.
  • Custom YouTube IDs and off-states are remembered.
