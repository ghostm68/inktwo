Setting & Geography · Alternate Present

Veridia: Sovereign Occult City-State

Veridia is an autonomous, treaty-chartered industrial-gothic port wedged between an oil-slicked Atlantic harbor and the quarantined desert of the Null-Wastes. Real-world European capitals—Paris, where the elite keep hidden estates, and Zurich, home to the banking syndicates behind the Iron Ledger—sit beyond its Firewall, connected to Veridia's underworld via covert Ley-Line corridors and encrypted coordinates. Above the smog, a decadent ruling class known as "the Leisure" trades in bottled emotions and stolen lifespan while the city below burns through its reserves.

01 // Tone & Genre (Click † terms for dossier notes)

Magic and Code, Written in Different Syntaxes

Prestige noir cyber-fantasy. A rain-slicked, decaying metropolis where analog occultism and digital architecture occupy the same skyline. Every spell leaves Dirty Magic in the atmosphere; every transaction carries a biological price, from the Zeno Effect to state-sanctioned Null-Static.

  • Political realism: Magic is regulated, taxed, and weaponized like energy infrastructure.
  • The cost of power: Every spell exacts a physical, biological, or moral toll—nothing is cast for free.
  • Corruption as infrastructure: The ruling class, enforcement agencies, and black markets are built into the same rotten foundation.
  • Hacker lexicon as theology: Incantations, blood pacts, and ancient vaults operate on the same logic as encryption, exploits, and root access.

What the audience feels: suspicion, then complicity. Every alliance is temporary. Every victory has a body count.

03 // Core Cast

Utilities, Traitors, and Living Ledgers

Protagonist · Age 32

Sloane Byrne

  • Former DOC agent, Project 9 "Garbage Collector," disavowed after eating a handler during a field operation.
  • Lives in the Dregs, dulling her ability with DOC-supplied suppressants.
  • Power: Ingests any form of energy—magic, thermal, null-static—and reprocesses it.
  • Cost: Her body is a living battery; overload burns her from the inside out.
  • Arc: From survivor → weapon → someone who chooses what she destroys.
Deuteragonist · Ageless (Appears 40s)

Ambrose Vane

  • Weaver: A high-tier mage who reshapes reality at a structural level.
  • Once the Coven's most effective enforcer; now a traitor carrying a century of unnamed guilt.
  • Cost: The "Zeno Effect"—his biology degrades with every spell, cracking like dry porcelain.
  • Arc: From self-serving pragmatist → someone who risks annihilation for something other than himself.
The Asset · Appears 10 (Not a Child)

Alice

  • Project 9's storage medium: a living blockchain.
  • Every spell, blood debt, and buried state secret in Veridia is encoded directly in her DNA.
  • Passive broadcaster—when the pressure builds, she "exhales" raw truth into the city's network.
  • Arc: From object → agent → the one person both factions forgot to ask for consent.
Antagonist · Age 50s

Director Halloway

  • Head of the Department of Occult Control (DOC).
  • Designed Sloane as a weapon; treats human lives as config files.
  • Believes Veridia's survival demands absolute information control.
  • Arc: Not a villain who grows worse—a villain who becomes more honest about what he already is.

05 // World Architecture

Rules of Veridia

Infrastructure

Magic as Code

Every spell is a transaction. Casting costs energy—drawn from the mage's biology or environmental reserves. Overdrawing produces "Dirty Magic": toxic waste runoff, oily smog, and systemic corruption.

Power Monopoly

The Coven of Leisure

The governing body of licensed mages—known on the streets as "the Leisure." They control the Ley-Line grid and mint Aether, measured in spell-output. Publicly, the First Law forbids consuming one's own kind; privately, the elite feed to stay immortal.

State Enforcement

The Bureau (DOC)

Government occult enforcement. They regulate, surveil, and sanitize rogue magic—while running black R&D programs like Project 9 that weaponized Sloane and caged Alice.

Myth & Sanctuary

The Tomb & The Null-Wastes

The Tomb isn't a vault—it's Alice, a pre-human biological ledger. Outside the city lies the Null-Wastes: a magic-dead, tech-hostile radioactive desert where neither the Coven nor the Bureau can track you.

002 Project 9 ("Garbage Collector") — Living anti-magic weapon engineered by DOC Director Halloway DISAVOWED
003 The Genesis Block / The Tomb — Pre-human biological ledger recording every spell, bribe, and blood debt ACTIVE NODE

06 // Multi-Season Engine & Production Brief

Three-Season Narrative Spine

Season 01

The Key

What is truth worth? If the ledger that could topple a corrupt city is a living child, can you still destroy it to save everyone else?

Season 02

The Ledger

What happens when the secret gets out? Once the public sees every crime committed in their name, do they build a new order—or eat each other alive?

Season 03

The Clean Slate

What survives after the system crashes? When both magic and authority collapse, can you build a world that isn't just a cleaner version of the same lie?

Season 01 // Six-Episode Arc ("The Key")
Episode 1x01 · Pilot

The Geometry of Hunger

When her murdered mentor sends a final encrypted brand into her flesh, disavowed Siphon Sloane Byrne crashes a dark-web auction—and is forced into an uneasy escape with the Coven enforcer who killed him.

Episode 1x02

Proof of Work

Bound by an immutable Blood Pact and hunted by the Bureau, Sloane and a decaying Ambrose rob an air-gapped vault run by Zurich Gnomes—discovering Garrick's "Key" leads to a hidden child in Sector 0.

Episode 1x03

Zero-Knowledge Proof

Trapped underground as Alice begins passively broadcasting the elite's buried crimes onto Veridia's billboards, Ambrose stages a high-stakes blackmail parley with the Countess while DOC hunter-killers close in.

Episode 1x04

The Double-Spend Problem

Fleeing into the magic-dead Null-Wastes with a dying Ambrose, Sloane must keep them alive using only analog grit—until Director Halloway deploys a mobile Reality Anchor that terraforms the desert behind them.

Episode 1x05

Quantum Tunneling

Cornered by silicon-eating nomads and DOC strike teams at a subterranean Firewall, Sloane and Ambrose breach the Source to save his failing life—at a devastating cost to his memory.

Episode 1x06 · Season Finale

The Genesis Block

Reinstated and stripped of his memories of Sloane, Ambrose delivers Alice to the Spire's grand rewrite ceremony. Left for dead, Sloane mounts a one-way assault to trigger an orphaned protocol—and force the system to remember.

Coven of Leisure is structured as a 1-hour serialized prestige drama (6–8 episodes per season across a 3-season arc). The Pilot teleplay (1x01), Expanded Series Bible, and full 3-season episode manuscripts are already drafted in-house and available upon request for collaborative development.

Veridia is an original, non-geolocked industrial gothic metropolis. Interior/exterior production can shoot cleanly across European, UK, Canadian, or Latin American tax-incentive hubs—pairing heritage brutalist/baroque architecture (Opera House, Iron Ledger bank, bridge markets) with minimal contained desert exteriors for the Null-Wastes.

Magic in this world operates through grounded, tactile physics—oily rain, practical lighting contrast, frozen-tableau camera staging, lead syringes, and porcelain skin prosthetics—keeping VFX targeted to high-impact signature moments rather than wall-to-wall green screen.
